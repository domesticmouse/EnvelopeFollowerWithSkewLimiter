## 4. Substitutions & Bring-Up Notes

- **`C1` / `C2` (`A-6764`):** Selected `A-6764` (`5 × 11 mm`, `2.0 ± 0.5 mm` straight pitch) over `A-7257` (`6.3 × 11 mm`, `5.0 mm` pre-formed leads) so caps seat flush into the `2.50 mm` PCB footprint without straightening leads.
- **`C12` / `C13` (`A-6940`):** Substituted KEMET R82 `1.5 µF / 63 V 5%` (`A-6940`) because Tayda `A-5753` (`2.2 µF 63 V`), `A-5786` (`2.2 µF 50 V`), and `A-5695` (`4.7 µF 50 V`) were all out of stock. Total `SLOW` capacitance (`C11 + C12 + C13`) is **`3.47 µF`** (`~7.4×` over `FAST`), shifting expected [`05-board-bringup.md`](../../checklists/05-board-bringup.md) `SLOW` times from `~3.0 s` rise / `~1.84 s` fall (CW) to **`~2.13 s` rise / `~1.31 s` fall** (and `~2.1 ms` / `~1.3 ms` at CCW).
- **Knobs & Pots:** Confirmed all four pots (`A-1855`, `A-1672`, `A-8401`) have `6.35 mm` smooth round shafts (`15R1`), matching `A-4559` (`6.35 mm` set screw, `12.7 mm` OD leaving `2.8 mm` clearance on `15.50 mm` pot pitch).
